About Me

I am a mechanical engineering undergraduate at UC San Diego with a concentration in fluid and thermal sciences. My interests focus on applying fundamental engineering principles to real-world design challenges involving fluid flow, thermal systems, and mechanical structures. Through coursework and hands-on projects, I have gained experience with CAD modeling in Fusion 360, as well as fabrication techniques, including CNC milling, machining, and metal fabrication.

Fall 2025 - Present

Design Build Fly - RC Aircraft

Objective: Design and optimize a mission-specific aircraft to compete at the American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics (AIAA) that maximizes competition score through efficient payload integration, aerodynamic performance, and reliable deployment systems.
Personal Contribution: Contributed to the fabrication and assembly of the aircraft, working with styrofoam, monokote, balsa, and birch wood, and creating precision laser-cut components using AutoCAD. Supported part integration, wiring, and iterative modifications during assembly to improve fit, manufacturability, and overall system reliability.
Results: Improved fabrication accuracy by accounting for a 0.06 in kerf in AutoCAD laser-cut designs and supported efficient assembly by organizing and managing laser-cut parts, contributing to smoother integration and reduced rework during the build process.

Fall 2025

CNC Handheld Projectile Launcher

Objective: Model and manufacture a working projectile launcher while practicing safe operating procedures of manual mills, lathes, band saws, and CNC milling and turning.
Personal Contribution: Programmed CNC toolpaths and machined launcher baseplate, brackets, and kinematic front and back from 303 stainless steel and PMMA stock.
Results: Successfully manufactured product with an average launching distance of 8ft with external and internal threads, fastener holes, bolt circle patterns, chamfered features, and a dimensional tolerance within ±0.010in across multiple machined components.

Spring 2024

Mechanical Pendulum Clock

Objective: Design and fabricate a pendulum-driven mechanical clock to analyze its timing performance using center of mass, moment of inertia, and oscillation period calculations.
Personal Contributions: Generated hand sketches and CAD models of clock components, fabricated parts using laser cutting and manual machining operations (drilling, reaming, tapping, press fits).
Results: Successfully fabricated and tested a pendulum-driven clock mechanism, achieving a 0.6s oscillation period and 7s escapement wheel revolution time.

Spring 2024

RC Projectile Robot

Objective: Design and fabricate a competition RC robot capable of collecting and delivering projectiles to designated scoring zones during a 60-second head-to-head robotics competition.
Personal Contribution: Designed and fabricated the drivetrain assembly on Fusion 360, integrating the wheel-axle system and traction components, and conducted torque and traction analysis to determine the motor requirements for mobility under payload conditions.
Results: Validated drivetrain performance through torque and traction analysis for a 1.77kg robot, calculating 0.022Nm required torque and measuring 0.292Nm motor torque, confirming sufficient drivetrain capability for robot mobility during competition operation.
